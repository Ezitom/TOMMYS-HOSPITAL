About TOMMY'S HOSPITAL

Founded on clinical excellence, serving families across Nigeria with empathy and top tier expertise.

Our Story

Founded in 2009, TOMMY'S HOSPITAL has been serving Lagos and the wider Nigerian community with compassionate, evidence-based healthcare. We are a full-service private hospital committed to dignity, access, and clinical excellence.

Over the past fifteen years, our medical facility has grown from a neighborhood clinic into a highly respected multidisciplinary medical center. We specialize in modern therapeutic techniques, reliable diagnosis, and primary medical support for all patient ages.

We pride ourselves on maintaining an inclusive, clean environment where patients are treated like family and received with warm care.

Mission, Vision, and Values

Our Mission

To provide affordable, dignified, and expert healthcare to every patient we serve.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted hospital in West Africa.

Our Values

Our daily clinical work is guided by four principles: Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, and Universal Access.
