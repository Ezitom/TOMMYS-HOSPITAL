Our Medical Services

Comprehensive care across every stage of life.

General & Family Medicine

Routine health checks, diagnosis, chronic care, immunizations, and general wellness consultation.

Emergency & Trauma Care

24/7 critical emergency response, ambulance dispatch, trauma management, and intensive care units.

Maternal & Child Health

Antenatal packages, clean delivery rooms, postnatal support, and expert pediatric clinical treatment.

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Accurate blood assays, chemical pathology, ultrasound scanning, and full health screening packages.

Surgery & Procedures

Elective and emergency surgical procedures, outpatient surgeries, and post-operative theater care.

Mental Health & Counseling

Therapy, psychiatric consultation, mood disorder management, and patient support groups.
